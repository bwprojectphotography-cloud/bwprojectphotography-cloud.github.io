In Between

The last project I put together was Apollo, my first ever concept project. The idea was shooting the moments that light chose. I've always been drawn to high contrast scenes, and I wanted to kind of let the light lead me to where it wanted to go. I like the concept of that idea, but it was kind of loose and impersonal.

Honestly, the whole reason I wanted to do it was just to bring an idea to life — from concept into reality. And I guess in that case, it did succeed. Everywhere else, it pretty much failed. I don't think the right photos went into it. I don't think enough time or dedication went into designing it. I slapped it together and put it out there.

But I think that is kind of how I am sometimes. I like to do broad strokes and then refine later. I needed to go through the process of planning shots, putting them together, getting them printed, and seeing it all come to life. Now that I've done it, I want to take it to another level and actually put more dedication into the entire book as a physical product. I think I have a more solid idea this time, and I can spend more time putting it together now that I'm familiar with the process.

This book is based on work that I've always done. It's still very much the type of photography I take, the type of street photography I take — but with a concept that is personal and very on theme with my life right now. The project is called In Between.
